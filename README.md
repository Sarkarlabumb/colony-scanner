# Colony Scanner — Quick User Guide

A phone-based tool for scanning mouse cage card barcodes and exporting the data straight into Excel. Works in **Safari on iPhone** (best supported right now — Android support is still being tested).

---

## Getting Started

1. Open the link: **[insert your GitHub Pages link here]**
2. Tap the **Share** icon in Safari → **Add to Home Screen**. This puts a mouse-icon shortcut on your phone so you don't have to type the link every time.

---

## Scanning a Cage Card

1. Tap **Start scanning**. Allow camera access if prompted.
2. Hold the card **6–8 inches away** from the phone, in good light, with the barcode centered in the box.
3. Hold steady and tap **Capture**. (Don't wait for it to scan automatically — the manual Capture button gives much more reliable results.)
4. The camera will briefly reset itself — this is normal, just wait a second.

### Confirm the Scan
- After a successful capture, you'll see the **Cage / Animal ID** it read.
- **Check that it's correct.** If it misread the number, just tap into the field and type the correct one — no need to start over.
- Tap **Continue**.

### Enter Cage Details
- Enter the number of **Males** and **Females** in the cage.
- If it's exactly **1 male + 1 female**, you'll be asked if it's a **breeding cage**.
  - If **Yes**, you can also enter **# of pups**, **pups' date of birth (MM/DD)**, and **litter number**.
- Don't have all the info handy? Tap **Skip for now** at any point — you can always fill it in later (see below).

---

## If a Barcode Won't Scan

Tap **Manual Entry** instead of using the camera. You'll get the same prompts (Cage ID → counts → breeding info), just without a barcode read — type the Cage/Animal ID in by hand.

---

## Editing Entries Later

Every scanned or manually-entered row appears in the list below the camera, and **every field is editable directly** — Cage ID, Notes, Males, Females, Breeding (Yes/No dropdown), Pups Count, Pups DOB, and Litter Number. Just tap into any field to correct or fill it in.

- **Delete row**: tap the link at the bottom of any entry.
- **Undo delete**: tap **Undo delete** in the toolbar (works for the most recent deletion only).
- **Clear all**: wipes every entry — use with care, this can't be undone.

---

## Exporting Your Data

Two buttons at the bottom of the screen:

- **Export .xlsx** — downloads a real Excel file, ready to open or merge into the master spreadsheet.
- **Export .csv** — a plain CSV version, if you'd rather paste into an existing sheet.
- **Share…** (if visible on your phone) — sends the Excel file straight to your phone's share sheet, so you can save it to Google Drive, OneDrive (if your university's IT allows it), AirDrop it, etc.

Your data stays saved in the app between visits (even if you close the browser), so you don't have to export after every single scan — batch it up and export when convenient.

---

## Quick Tips

- **Distance matters more than steadiness on iPhone** — 6–8 inches, good even lighting, and the barcode filling most of the guide box works best.
- If a capture keeps failing, try backing off the distance slightly or reducing glare (angle the card away from overhead lights).
- The build number in the top right of the app (e.g. "build 2026-07-30-3") tells you which version you're on — handy if we're troubleshooting something together.
